# 15D input ablation results

90 runs. Mean ± sample std over init seeds (song split fixed at seed 42). **ρ KK** = Spearman(latent distance, Krumhansl & Kessler 1982 interkey distance) over the 15 pairs of I ii iii IV V vi (primary). **ρ legacy** = same against the hand-entered matrix of eval_cognitive_comparison.py (21 pairs). Δ/p compare each variant with full15 on ρ KK (Welch t-test, Mann-Whitney U). **sig** = runs whose own exact Mantel test (all chord-label permutations, one-sided) gives p < 0.05. Test TPS ρ = Spearman(latent step length, TPS target) over all adjacent transitions of the held-out test split.
